Force Balance Bar

Persistent top-of-map widget showing Blue vs Red unit counts, total health, and assets destroyed. Updates live as the sim ticks. Visual indicator of who's winning at a glance.

D

Mission Planning & Orders

D1

Drag-to-Assign Strike UI

In "strike mode," blue assets pulse as available shooters. Drag from a blue asset to a red asset to queue a strike — no context menu required. A ghost arc previews the trajectory while dragging. Replaces the current right-click flow with a more spatial, direct-manipulation model.

D2

Mission Queue Panel

A collapsible side panel showing all queued and in-flight missions in order. Supports reordering by drag, cancellation, and priority bumping. Shows estimated tick completion. Complements the existing strike log which shows completed missions.

D3

Waypoint & Patrol Route Editor

Click on the map to place waypoints; assign them to a friendly asset to define a patrol or movement route. Replaces the current single-destination move order with a multi-waypoint system. Routes persist until cleared or the asset is destroyed.
